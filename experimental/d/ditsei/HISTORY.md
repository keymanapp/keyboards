Ditsei Change History
=====================

0.1 (2026-09-11)
-----------------
* Initial version, forked from the Cabécar keyboard (release/c/cabecar) by
  Victoria Quint
* Added low tone (grave) vowel forms (à, è, ì, ò, ù and uppercase)
* Reworked desktop dead-key rules to precompose a mark when it's the first
  one applied to a vowel, and append a combining mark on top when a vowel
  already carries another mark, so tone/nasal/dieresis marks can be typed
  in any order and stack correctly
* Added one-press shortcuts for dieresis+tone and nasal+tone combinations
* Added touch-layout long-press keys for high and low tone marks
