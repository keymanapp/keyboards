Romania keyboard for Romanian language limba română.

This is an innovative Romanian keyboard for phone and computer.

This keyboard can automatically remain the "î" letter in the beginning and the end of a word and transfer the "î" letter to "â" letter in the middle of a sentence.

You can type "î" key and get "â" automatically, depending on the content.

You can still type "â" individually as usual

You can still type "î" specifically by Alt + I. In this way, the keyboard will remain the spelling with "î" letter.

You can type "ă" "â" "î" "ș" "ț" by long-pressing the "a" "i" "s" "t" key on the phone and pad as usual.
