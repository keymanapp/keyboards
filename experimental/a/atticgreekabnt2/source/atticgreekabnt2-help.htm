Attic Greek for ABNT2: Quick Help

Press accent keys before typing the vowel. The order of accent keys does not matter.

Main Accent Keys:

  • (ç) : Iota subscript
  • (~) : Tilde
  • (´) : Acute / Grave accents
  • ([) : Rough breathing
  • (]) : Soft breathing
  • (-) : Macron
