# Kom (Cameroon) keyboard

A keyboard for writing Kom (Grassfields, Cameroon; language code bkm) on phones, tablets and computers.

## The Kom alphabet

a, ae, b, ch, d, e, f, g, gh, i, ɨ, j, ’ (glottal stop), k, l, m, n, ny, ŋ, o, oe, s, t, u, ue, v, w, y, z

Tone marks: low tone (grave, as in à) and falling tone (circumflex, as in â).

## Typing on a phone or tablet

* ae, oe, ue, ch, gh and ny each have their own key.
* Press and hold i to get ɨ and tone marks on i and ɨ.
* Press and hold n to get ŋ.
* Press and hold a, e, i, o or u to get the low and falling tone marks.
* The ’ key next to the space bar types the glottal stop.
* The 123 key shows digits and punctuation.
* Shift gives capitals: AE, OE, UE, Ch, Gh, Ny, as in the alphabet table.

The keyboard shows only the letters of the Kom alphabet.

## Typing on a computer

* AltGr + I gives ɨ (with Shift, Ɨ)
* AltGr + N gives ŋ (with Shift, Ŋ)
* AltGr + apostrophe key gives ’
* AltGr + backtick key puts a low tone mark on the vowel before it
* AltGr + 6 puts a falling tone mark on the vowel before it
