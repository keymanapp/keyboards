Kom keyboard

Welcome! This keyboard lets you write Kom (Grassfields, Cameroon) on your phone, tablet or computer.

The Kom alphabet

a, ae, b, ch, d, e, f, g, gh, i, ɨ, j, ’, k, l, m, n, ny, ŋ, o, oe, s, t, u, ue, v, w, y, z

Tone marks: low tone (à) and falling tone (â).

On a phone or tablet

To typeDo this
ae, oe, ue, ch, gh, nyTap the key with that name
ɨPress and hold i, then choose ɨ
ŋPress and hold n, then choose ŋ
Low or falling tonePress and hold the vowel, then choose it
’ (glottal stop)Tap the ’ key next to the space bar
Numbers and punctuationTap 123
Capital lettersTap the Shift key

On a computer

To typePress
ɨAltGr + I
ŋAltGr + N
’AltGr + apostrophe key
Low tone on the vowel beforeAltGr + backtick key
Falling tone on the vowel beforeAltGr + 6
