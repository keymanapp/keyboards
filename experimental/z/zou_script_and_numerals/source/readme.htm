Zou Script and Numerals

This is the first Zou digital keyboard designed, created, edited and published by Dr. T Marvin Zou. Dr. T Marvin Zou belongs to the Zou community from Tibeto Burman. This Zou digital keyboard is free to use for all users to enhance learning, research and knowledge related to the Zou community. It includes the 27 Zou letters, Zou numerals, and added vowel signs, special signs, and punctuation marks mapped in the Private Use Area.

Since the script is not encoded in Unicode, the codepoints in this keyboard are using Private Use Area Unicode codepoints. When, and if, the script is encoded in the Unicode, those codepoints will change.

Keyboard Overview

The Zou script consists of 27 alphabets, Zou numerals, and additional vowel signs, special signs, and punctuation marks. Due to the 26-key limit of the standard English keyboard, specific adjustments have been made to include the 27th letter, numerals, vowel signs, special signs, and punctuation marks.
