Nepali MPP Traditional Change History
=====================================

1.0.0 (2026-10-05)
------------------
* Initial release of authentic Nepali Traditional layout conforming strictly to the official Madan Puraskar Pustakalaya (MPP) & Language Technology Kendra (LTK) specification.
* Includes all standard conjunct keys:
  - ज्ञ (Shift+1), द्ध (Shift+4), त्र (q), त्त (Shift+Q), ड्ढ (Shift+W), द्व (Shift+R), ट्ट (Shift+T), ठ्ठ (Shift+Y), क्ष (Shift+I)
  - र् (Reph on '['), ृ (Matra Ri on '{')
  - ङ्क (Shift+S), ङ्ग (Shift+D), द्द (Shift+G), ट्ठ (Shift+;), क्क (Shift+Z), ह्म (Shift+X), द्य (Shift+N), ड्ड (Shift+M)
  - ः (Visarga on 'm')
  - ऽ (Avagraha on ','), श्र (Shift+.), रु (Shift+/)
* Fully supports ZWJ (U+200D on '=') and ZWNJ (U+200C on '+').
