Nepali MPP Romanized Change History
===================================

1.0.0 (2026-10-05)
------------------
* Initial release of authentic Nepali Romanized layout conforming to the official Madan Puraskar Pustakalaya (MPP) & Language Technology Kendra (LTK) specification.
* Includes Shift+4 ligature for 'रु.' (U+0930 U+0941 U+002E).
* Fully supports ZWJ (U+200D on '=') and ZWNJ (U+200C on '+').
* Supports multi-platform desktop, web, and mobile touch layouts.
