Ibibio Tonal Keyboard Change History
====================================

1.2 (24 Oct 2026)
-----------------
* Touch: smaller 4-row layout. ị at the end of the top row, ọ ʌ at the end of the middle row, ñ next to m
* Touch: one tone key (ˊˋ) by the space bar: tap 1-5 times for high, low, falling, rising, downstep; 6 removes it
* Touch: long-press any vowel, m, n or ñ for toned letters (stored as letter + combining mark, same as the tone key)
* Touch: comma key next to the space bar; long-press . for ' ? ! -
* Touch: 123 and #+= layers laid out like the iPhone keyboard; space or Enter there returns to the letters
* Touch: tones work after capitals too
* Removed ụ (not used in Ibibio) from touch and desktop (u ; now types u;)
* New step-by-step "How to Type Ibibio" guide (welcome page and help)

-----------------
* Add an on-screen keyboard for desktop and web that labels the under-dot and tone keys
