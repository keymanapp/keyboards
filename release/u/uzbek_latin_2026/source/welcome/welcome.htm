Uzbek Latin 2026

This keyboard types the Uzbek Latin alphabet as amended in 2026 (Law No. ORQ-1184 of 21 October 2026): the letters ö ğ ş ç and the tutuq ʼ (U+02BC MODIFIER LETTER APOSTROPHE).

Note: the desktop layout follows the layout proposed at the Senate on 10 September 2026. It is not an official standard. If an official standard is published later, the keyboard will be updated.

Desktop

  • Caps Lock gives Ö Ğ Ş Ç, as for the other letters.
  • The tutuq ʼ is the same with or without Shift, so it can be typed inside a word in capitals (e.g. MASʼUL).
  • The characters [ ] ; ' \ and { } : " | have moved to the Right Alt key (AltGr), on the same keys.

Phone and tablet

  • Phone: ö ğ ş ç ʼ are on their own row above the letters.
  • Tablet: the keys are where they are on the desktop: ö ğ ʼ after P, ş ç after L.
  • On both, you can also touch and hold o, g, s or c to get ö, ğ, ş or ç.
  • Touch and hold the full stop for , ? ! : ; - « » " '.
  • The 123 key opens numbers and symbols, including [ ] { } \ | ; : ' ".
