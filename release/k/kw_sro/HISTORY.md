KW SRO Change History
=====================

1.0 (2026-11-07)
----------------
* Created by Kevin Waddell, based on the FirstVoices Cree - Roman Orthography keyboard 10.2.
* `[`, `]`, and `\` type themselves unless they follow a vowel.
* To type `\`, `]`, `[`, or `` ` `` right after a vowel, press that key twice.
* To replace a vowel's mark with a different one, press the key for the new mark: ā then `]` gives â.
* y then `[` gives ý.
* The phone and tablet layout has every letter, a ◌̄ key that adds a mark to the letter you just typed, and a hyphen key.
* Nakawē (Saulteaux): s then `\` gives š and c then `\` gives č, for writers who use them; on touch, hold s or c, or use ◌̌.
* East Cree and Stoney Nakoda: u then `]` gives û.
* Algonquin and Mohawk: a vowel then `` ` `` gives a grave (à); on touch, hold the vowel, or use ◌̀.
