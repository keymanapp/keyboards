KW SRO keyboard
===============

Description
-----------
Standard Roman Orthography (SRO) for Plains, Woods, Swampy, and East Cree, and for Nakawē (Saulteaux).

Type a vowel, then `\` for a macron (ā ē ī ō ū) or `]` for a circumflex (â ê î ô û).
`[` gives an acute (á é í ó ú), and `y` then `[` gives ý.
`` ` `` gives a grave (à è ì ò ù), for Algonquin and Mohawk.
Some Nakawē writers also use š and č: type `s` or `c`, then `\`, as in the FirstVoices Anishinaabemowin keyboard.
For East Cree and Stoney Nakoda, `u` then `]` gives û.
Every other key types as on a US keyboard.
On phones and tablets, hold a letter for its marked forms, or tap ◌̄ after a vowel (hold ◌̄ for ◌̂, ◌́, ◌̀, and ◌̌).

Based on the FirstVoices Cree - Roman Orthography keyboard (fv_cree_latin). In this keyboard:

* The caron (š, č) is on `\` after s or c, for the Nakawē writers who use it.
* `[`, `]`, and `\` type themselves when they don't follow a vowel.
* To type `\`, `]`, `[`, or `` ` `` right after a vowel, press that key twice.
* To replace a vowel's mark with a different one, press the key for the new mark: ā then `]` gives â.
* `` ` `` after a vowel gives a grave (à è ì ò ù), for Algonquin and Mohawk.

Languages
---------
* Cree (Latin), including Woods Cree
* Plains Cree (Latin)
* Swampy Cree (Latin)
* Northern East Cree (Latin)
* Southern East Cree (Latin)
* Nakawē (Saulteaux)
* Stoney Nakoda
* Algonquin
* Mohawk
