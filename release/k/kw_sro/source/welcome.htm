KW SRO keyboard

For Plains, Woods, Swampy, and East Cree, and for Nakawē (Saulteaux), in Standard Roman Orthography. It also works for Stoney Nakoda, Algonquin, and Mohawk.

On a computer

Type the letter, then the key for the mark you want.

  • Some Nakawē writers use š and č: type s or c, then \.
  • For East Cree and Stoney Nakoda, u then ] gives û.
  • The grave (à) is for Algonquin and Mohawk.
  • Capitals work the same way: A \ gives Ā.
  • To replace a mark with a different one, press the key for the new mark: ā then ] gives â.
  • Backspace takes off the mark first.
  • Every other key types as usual.

On a phone or tablet

  • Hold a letter for its marked forms: a for ā, â, á, and à; s for š; c for č.
  • Or tap ◌̄ after a vowel; hold it for ◌̂, ◌́, ◌̀, or ◌̌ (after s or c).
  • Tap ◌̄ again to take the mark off.
  • The hyphen is beside the space bar.
