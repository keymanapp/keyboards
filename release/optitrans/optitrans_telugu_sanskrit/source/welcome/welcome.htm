Description

Telugu Sanskrit Phonetic (OPTITRANS) keyboard is telugu-sanskrit input method by a modified version of OPTITRANS and Harvard-Kyoto transliteration systems.

You can use all the standard ITRANS key sequences plus key sequences such as the below.

nk->ఙ్క్, nkh->ఙ్ఖ్, ng->ఙ్గ్, ngh->ఙ్ఘ్ nch->ఞ్చ్, nCh->ఞ్ఛ్, nc->ఞ్చ్, nC->ఞ్ఛ్, nchh->ఞ్ఛ్, nj->ఞ్జ్, njh->ఞ్ఝ్, nT->ణ్ట్, nTh->ణ్ఠ్, nD->ణ్డ్, nDh->ణ్ఢ్ c->చ్, C->ఛ్, z->జ఼్, S->ష్, jn->జ్ఞ్, R->ఋ

Examples: Type saMskRta for సంస్కృత.

Consonantal Diacritics

Arranged with the vowels are two consonantal diacritics, the final nasal anusvāra ం M and the final fricative visarga ః H (called అం aṃ and అః aḥ). Another diacritic used in other languages written in Telugu script is the candrabindu/anunāsika ఁ MM (అఁ). These consonantal diacritics follow the vowel signs including the implicit `a`. The following table shows consonant `k` followed by various dependent vowel signs and consonantal diacritics ం and ః.

Conjuncts

Consonant conjuncts are automatically formed, e.g. k t produces క్త్‌.

Explicit Virama

To force an explicit virama at end of word, use .h e.g. k t .h space produces క్త్‌ .

Numbers

  1. Press the number keys to type Telugu digits. for example 9 produces ౯.
  2. For typing the Arabic digits, press the number keys and then the backspace key, for example 9 Back space produces 9.
