Dinka (DILDA) Keyboard

A keyboard layout for the Dinka (Thuɔŋjäŋ) language, based on the Grammar of Unified Thuɔŋjäŋ published by DILDA (Akutmɛ̈t Latueŋ Thuɔŋjäŋ), Juba, South Sudan, 2021.

The unified writing system was developed through workshops organized by DILDA between 2011 and 2019, with representatives from all Thuɔŋjäŋ dialect groups (Agar, Bor, Padang, and Rek), and consultation from Prof. John Myhill of the University of Haifa.
